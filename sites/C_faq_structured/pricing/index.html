Pricing FAQ

Q: What does an RTX 4090 GPU cost if I need it not to be interrupted?

A: On-demand RTX 4090 costs $0.99 per hour. The preemptible price ($0.57 per hour) can be interrupted.

Q: How do I calculate the cost of a CPU sandbox?

A: Add vCPU count x $0.0504/h, plus memory GiB x $0.0162/h, plus disk GiB above the first 5 free GiB x $0.000108/h.

Q: Do I need a credit card to start?

A: No. The free trial requires no credit card and includes $200 in free compute.

Q: How is usage billed?

A: All billing is calculated per second.

Q: How much does a Windows sandbox cost?

A: Windows costs $0.0858 per vCPU per hour.
